## RideSync v1.1.0

**Accounts and cloud sync.** Sign in with Google, and your rides, statistics and preferences follow you to any phone. Rides finished offline now upload by themselves.

**Download:** `RideSync-v1.1.0.apk` under **Assets** below (Android). Verify it with `RideSync-v1.1.0.apk.sha256`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Upgrading from v1.0.0:** this version is signed with a different key, so Android cannot install it over v1.0.0. Uninstall v1.0.0 first, then install v1.1.0. Rides saved in v1.0.0 belonged to an anonymous session on that phone and cannot be carried over after uninstalling. From v1.1.0 on, rides belong to your Google account.

### 🚀 New Features

- **Google Sign-In** with a single "Continue with Google" button.
- **Your own rider name:** chosen after the first sign-in and used on group rides, the leaderboard, history and share cards. Your Google name is never shown.
- **Profile page** with your picture, rider name, email, member since and personal statistics.
- **Edit Profile** to change your rider name and solo ride colour.
- **Solo rider colour** for your dot, trail, replay, history and ride cards.
- **Cloud ride history** that restores on any phone you sign in on.
- **Sign Out** from the profile page.

### ✨ Improvements

- **Settings sync:** rider name, solo colour and the fuel layer setting follow you across devices.
- **Offline ride upload:** a ride finished without a connection is saved on the phone, queued, and uploaded automatically when you are back online, even after the app is closed.
- **Cloud sync status** on the profile (✓ Synced, Syncing..., Offline) and a **Sync Now** button.
- **Home** greets you by rider name and has a profile button.
- **Returning riders** go straight to Home on launch.

### 🐞 Bug Fixes

- **Rider names** are never cleared by signing in again on another phone.
- **The saving screen** now explains when the phone is offline, instead of only showing "Saving ride...".
- **Session changes** never interrupt a ride in progress.

### ⚡ Performance

- **Personal statistics** are calculated on the phone from your ride history, with no extra data stored in the cloud.
- **Profile and preference saves** apply on the phone at once and upload in the background.
- **Routing** reacts only to the profile changes that matter for it, so editing your profile never reloads screens.

### 🔐 Authentication

- **Google Sign-In** through Firebase Authentication.
- **Your data belongs to your account:** profile, preferences and ride history are stored under your Firebase user ID.
- **Firestore security rules** let each rider read and write only their own profile, with only the known profile fields and preference types. Shared group ride data keeps its existing rules.

### 🗺 Navigation

- **Rides are never interrupted** by sign-in prompts: the lobby, planning, the live map and results stay open.
- **Your solo colour** draws your trail and replay on the map.
- Guided navigation, off-route help and the live map work as in v1.0.0.

### ⛽ Fuel Stations

- **The fuel layer setting** (on or off) now syncs with your account across devices.
- Brand markers, clustering and the station sheet work as in v1.0.0.

### 📱 UI Improvements

- **New sign-in screen** in the RideSync dark style.
- **"What should we call you?"** rider-name screen after the first sign-in.
- **Profile and Edit Profile** pages with a stats grid, sync pill and colour picker.
- **Profile avatar** on Home, showing your Google picture or your initial.

### 📊 Ride Analytics

- **Totals:** rides, distance and ride time across your whole history.
- **Records:** highest speed, longest ride, average distance and average speed (weighted by moving time).
- **Rides by type:** guided, free roam, group and solo.

### 📲 Installing

1. Uninstall v1.0.0 if it is installed.
2. Download `RideSync-v1.1.0.apk` from the assets below and open it on your Android phone.
3. Allow installing from this source if asked.
4. Sign in with Google, choose your rider name, and grant location (including background) and notification permissions.
